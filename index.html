FAQ

Clear answers before you buy.

Transparent information about pricing, platforms, BYOK and activation.

What is Dark Bot AI?

Dark Bot AI is a cross-platform AI application built around HINA, your AI assistant for coding, research, productivity, creative work and technical workflows.

What does the $25 payment include?

The $25 one-time purchase is for the Dark Bot AI application/product tier. Third-party AI model/API usage is separate.

What does BYOK mean?

BYOK means Bring Your Own Key. You connect your own supported AI provider credentials and pay that provider directly for usage.

How do I receive the application?

During the launch MVP, official support verifies your payment and provides the appropriate Windows, Linux or Android application package.

How does activation work?

Open the app to view your unique Installation ID, send it to official support, receive an activation key, and enter it in the app.

Can I move my license to another device?

Device replacement and re-activation can be handled by support under the final device policy.

Which platforms are available?

Dark Bot AI is currently available for Windows, Linux and Android.

Is AI/API usage included?

No. The application purchase does not include third-party model/API credits or provider charges. Those costs are paid directly by the user to the selected provider.

Is Dark Bot AI intended for illegal or harmful activity?

No. The product is intended for lawful and authorized use.
