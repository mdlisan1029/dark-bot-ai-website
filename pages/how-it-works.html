ACTIVATION

From purchase to HINA

  1. Purchase the $25 lifetime application.
  2. Send order/payment information to official Telegram support.
  3. Support verifies the payment and provides the app.
  4. Open the app and copy the Installation ID.
  5. Send the ID to support.
  6. Support generates a signed activation key.
  7. Enter the key and activate Dark Bot AI.
Launch-stage approach: manual activation keeps the first version simple. Automation can be added later.
